# bank-loan-risk-stress-testing-system
Built an end-to-end Bank Loan Portfolio Risk & Stress Testing System replicating real-world workflows used by Tier-1 banks and Big 4 firms.

Developed a fully dynamic risk engine where portfolio-level capital impact recalculates in real time through scenario-driven stress testing.

📊 Key Highlights:
Processed 5,000+ loan records with complete data cleaning, encoding, and standardization pipeline

Designed a Basel-inspired Credit Risk Scorecard for borrower risk classification

Built a Probability of Default (PD) model ranging from 2%–18%

Implemented IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) framework:

ECL = PD × LGD × EAD

Created a live stress-testing simulator (Base / Mild / Severe scenarios)

Developed an interactive executive dashboard for decision-making insights

⚙️ Tech & Frameworks:
Excel · Risk Modeling · Basel III · IFRS 9 · Credit Risk Analytics

🚀 Outcome:
Enabled real-time capital impact analysis under stress scenarios

Simulated workflows aligned with enterprise-level risk management systems

Achieved 17.5% approval rate optimization based on risk thresholds
